## Candidate metadata discrepancy

The suggested "Taylor and Howe (2007)" citation has a conflicting [NIST catalog record](https://www.nist.gov/publications/theoh-and-allan-deviation-power-law-noise-estimators-0). The [published IEEE paper hosted by NIST](https://tf.nist.gov/general/pdf/2116.pdf) names **Jennifer A. McGee and David A. Howe**, prints pages 448–452, and gives DOI 10.1109/TUFFC.2007.260. The bibliography uses the published-paper metadata under `mcgee2007`, not the suggested Taylor attribution. The optional data-gap paper was not added because it is not needed for the claims revised here.
