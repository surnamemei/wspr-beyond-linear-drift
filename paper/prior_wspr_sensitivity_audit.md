# Prior WSPR sensitivity source audit

The values below are source-reported quantities, not interchangeable estimates of the present production-WSJT-X-3.0.2 `wsprd` SNR50. The present SNR50 is an interpolated 50% exact-message decode point under the repository's injected 2500-Hz AWGN convention.

Comparison discipline: nominal sensitivity, a lowest successful decode, an author's practical threshold, and an interpolated 50% decode-probability point are different estimands. Matching the printed dB reference alone cannot resolve differences in decoder version, search configuration, generated waveform, noise implementation, payload, fading/Doppler assumptions, or threshold definition. These sources establish earlier WSPR sensitivity context, not a like-for-like numerical benchmark against the present SNR50 of -31.519 dB.
