# Graphical abstract plan

The draft at `figures/graphical_abstract_draft.png` is an original conceptual diagram generated by `figures/build_graphical_abstract.py`. It depicts an imposed frequency trajectory, removal of the decoder-representable constant and linear components, residual frequency, a severity/shape descriptor, production WSJT-X `wsprd`, and an observed decode-probability boundary. Its small annotations indicate contraction near the weak-signal threshold and possible differences among tested trajectory families. It includes no unmeasured numerical values, hardware claim, or universal oscillator requirement.

The draft is 1320 x 590 px to keep source text readable. If the article is accepted, adapt a final JPG near 660 x 295 px and below 45 KB, and confirm that it is drawn from a figure/image in the accepted article. This final-file requirement appears in the [IEEE Access post-acceptance guide](https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/post-acceptance-guide/). The current draft is not a submission blocker or a replacement for any of the seven scientific figures.
