The LaTeX file stages the seven numbered figures using the paper figures directory, including both lettered source panels for Figs. 2, 3, and 6. Figure numbers, `P_decode` captions, SNR units (dB), and frequency units (Hz or MHz as applicable) are consistent in manuscript text. Existing source graphics retain some different plain-language typography; no data-driven figure was regenerated merely to change notation. The compiled PDF shows no missing graphics or visibly clipped labels in the inspected pages. Fig. 7's shared legend remains outside the axes.

Remaining production issues: the TeX log has two overfull vertical boxes (73.40004 pt and 5.5157 pt) associated with the tall paired-figure pages. The rendered pages show no obvious cropping, but float height and bottom-margin geometry need final venue-proof review. Fig. 2 cell annotations and Figs. 4/6 small legend/tick text should be checked at actual print size before submission. The bibliography and equations render, with no remaining horizontal overflow after line-break fixes.
